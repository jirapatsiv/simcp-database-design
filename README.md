# SIMCP Database Design

## Overview

The **State Integrated Mobility Charging Platform (SIMCP)** is a conceptual digital platform designed to provide a centralised system for managing mobility-related services.

The platform aims to support different types of users, including individual customers, families, and business organisations, while providing a single account structure that can be connected to vehicles, authorised devices, concession eligibility, and business fleets.

The broader goal of SIMCP is to create an integrated mobility ecosystem where users can manage their mobility profile and related services through one platform rather than relying on multiple disconnected systems.

As part of this project, I focused on designing the relational database structure required to support the **Mobility Account Platform (Module 1)**.

## My Contribution

My main responsibilities included:

* Analysing system requirements
* Identifying entities and relationships
* Designing the ERD
* Defining primary and foreign keys
* Applying database normalisation
* Designing junction tables for many-to-many relationships
* Supporting data integrity and historical tracking

## Entity Relationship Diagram

The database contains **25 relational tables** across several key areas:

* Mobility Account Management
* Vehicle Management
* Business Account & Fleet Management
* Family Group Management
* Authorised Device Management
* Concession Eligibility
